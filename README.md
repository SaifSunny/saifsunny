## About me

I'm a **PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of East London** and a **Data Analyst at Brother Marcus** in London. I build predictive models, decision-support dashboards and web applications that help people use data in their everyday work.

My work brings together **applied machine learning, explainable AI and software engineering**, with projects in healthcare, restaurant operations, Bangla natural language processing and financial analytics. I'm interested in how models perform beyond a notebook: how they are evaluated, explained and used by the people making decisions.

## Current work

- **Restaurant decision support:** predicting operational delays, explaining forecasts with SHAP and developing dashboards for service performance.
- **Healthcare analytics:** modelling emergency department delays and admission risk, alongside knowledge graph research on unplanned hospital visits.
- **Scientific machine learning:** developing lightweight reduced-order emulators for three-layer ocean simulations.
