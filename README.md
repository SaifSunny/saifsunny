## About me

I'm **Saieef Sarower Sunny**, a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the **University of East London** and a **Data Analyst at Brother Marcus**. I build predictive models, explainable decision-support systems and web applications that turn complex data into useful tools.

My interests span **applied machine learning, healthcare analytics, natural language processing, computer vision and scientific modelling**. I enjoy the whole process: understanding the data, testing a model carefully, explaining its behaviour and building an application people can use.

## Research & applied AI

| Focus | Work |
| --- | --- |
| **Restaurant decision support** | Service-delay prediction using XGBoost, SHAP explanations and dashboards for operational decisions. |
| **Restaurant AI agents** | Gemini-orchestrated analytics agents connecting natural-language questions to POS data, KPIs and automated reporting. |
| **Healthcare analytics** | Emergency department delay and admission-risk modelling, plus knowledge graphs exploring unplanned hospital visits. |
| **Scientific machine learning** | Lightweight reduced-order emulators for three-layer ocean simulations. |
| **Computer vision** | Plant identification with CNNs and leaf-disease classification using VGG16 transfer learning. |
| **Sustainable finance** | EcoTrack: transaction-based carbon tracking and sustainability insights, developed with my hackathon team. |
