## About me

I'm **Saieef Sarower Sunny**, a **PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of East London**. My work brings together three areas: **research, AI and machine learning engineering, and software engineering**.

I design and evaluate experiments, build models that learn from data, and develop the applications and APIs that make those models useful. My industry work at **Brother Marcus**, where I work as a Data Analyst, connects data analysis and business intelligence with applied AI, explainable models and decision-support software.

## Three areas of work

| Area | What I do | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Researcher** | Develop research questions, curate datasets, compare methods, evaluate results and communicate findings. | NHS research, Bangla NLP publications, open datasets and reduced-order ocean modelling. |
| **AI & ML Engineer** | Build predictive and deep learning models, explain their outputs and connect them to practical decision-making. Data analytics, feature engineering and dashboards support this work. | Restaurant delay prediction, Gemini-based analytics agents, healthcare risk modelling, plant disease classification and EcoTrack. |
| **Software Engineer** | Build web applications, APIs, database-backed systems and integrations that connect users with data and services. | EveCode, the World Bank Data API, Sabre-based travel booking and AI-enabled applications. |
